# The Sneaky Pivot Strategy (The Rumers — Doug)

## What It's About
26-year professional trader Doug teaches a single dead-simple day trading strategy called the **Sneaky Pivot**. One timeframe, no indicators, four price levels drawn from yesterday's price action, ~15 minutes of actual screen time per day. 554K+ views — one of the most-watched simple trading videos out there.

## The Core Idea
> "Every time I tried to get smarter, cuter, or more clever with the market, it always made me look stupid."

Overcomplication kills trading. One timeframe (15-min) + one pattern (3-candle pivot) + four price levels = consistent profitability.

## The Setup — 4 Magic Lines

Draw 4 horizontal lines from the **previous day's price action**:

| Line | What It Is |
|------|-----------|
| **Swing High** | Significant high above yesterday's high |
| **Range High** | Yesterday's high |
| **Range Low** | Yesterday's low |
| **Swing Low** | Significant low below yesterday's low |

**TradingView shortcut:** Search "Rumors Magic Lines" — auto-plots all four. Free indicator.

## The Rules
- ✅ Only **BUY** at Range Low or Swing Low
- ✅ Only **SELL** at Range High or Swing High
- ❌ Do **nothing** in between — no trades in the middle

## The 3-Candle Entry (The Sneaky Pivot)

```
Candle 1 (0:00–0:15): Strong move INTO one of the four levels
Candle 2 (0:15–0:30): Reverses — confirms the level held (the "sneaky" candle)
Candle 3 (0:30–0:45): ENTRY — crosses above/below the sneaky candle's high/low
```

Entry typically happens at the **45-minute mark** of the trading day.

## Stop Loss
- Long: below the Swing Low / Range Low
- Short: above the Swing High / Range High
- Logic: institutional order flow protects these levels — if they break, trade is invalid

## Profit Target
Price travels from one extreme to the other — buy at the bottom, target the top (Range High → Swing High). Range-bound strategy.
